# Residue is upstream of the creatures — the two-arrow chain, decided

**Status: DECIDED 2026-10-21.** Fiction decision. No mechanism change, no code change, nothing to
implement. This settles one sub-question of
[`2026-10-20-residue-terrain-derivation-design.md`](2026-10-20-residue-terrain-derivation-design.md);
**that document remains a PROPOSED DIRECTION in every other respect**, and the three committed residue
specs it would supersede are still un-retired.

**Provenance tiers** are the ones that document defines: **[measured]**, **[jar]**, **[lore-1p]**,
**[lore]**, **[proposed]**. This decision rests on [lore-1p] and [jar]; it introduces no new readings
of its own.

---

## The decision

Residue sits **upstream of the game's creatures**, not beside them:

```
terrain  →  residue field (ours)  →  creatures (the game's)  →  essence items (the game's)
```

Residue is the **field**. Essence items are what the field's creatures yield. Terrain shapes residue,
residue explains why those creatures live where they do, creatures drop what the player collects.

The mod therefore supplies the **missing causal first term** for content the game already ships, rather
than asserting a parallel source of essence.

## What it changes

Nothing mechanical. §5's derivation table — Fire at lava, Water at rivers and ice, Earth in stone, Wind
at exposed heights, Lightning at storm-frequent environments × exposure — is **unchanged**. What changes
is what the derived field *means*.

There is also **no code to change**, verified 2026-10-21: `elementId` is an opaque `ResourceId` with no
enum, no list and no validation, and every `DefaultResidueNetwork(seed, elementId, params)` construction
in the repository is in a test. Production consumers: zero.

## Why

Three reasons, in order of weight.

**1. It is the only framing consistent with [`CLAUDE.md`](../../../CLAUDE.md) § "Lore first."** Lore
speaks plainly about where essence comes from, and it does not say *ground*: Fire *"lingers in the
creatures that dwell amid the desolated volcanic regions"*; Lightning's *"elementals born from its
lingering traces still wander the lands"*; Water *"as water filters through their gills, the
concentration grows"*; Life *"found in abundance around plant life on Orbis"*. [lore-1p] §5 as written
had quietly answered differently by treating the field as where essence *is*. Where lore speaks, we
follow.

**2. The engine's own creature-placement predicate reads the same signals the derivation does.** [jar]
`NPCSpawn` exposes `getEnvironments()`, `getLightRange(LightType)`, `getDayTimeRange()`,
`getMoonPhaseRange()`, and `RoleSpawnParameters.getSpawnBlockSet()` — so the spawner's rule is

```
environment  ×  sky-light range  ×  block set  ×  time of day / moon phase
```

whose three *spatial* inputs are the ones §5 already derives residue from. A field built on those
signals agrees with creature placement **by construction**, which makes the fiction near-isomorphic with
the engine's own rule rather than merely compatible with it. This argument did not exist when the
framing was first proposed; it arrived with the 2026-10-21 spawn-table join and is the decisive one.

**3. It converts the roster's weakest point into content.** [jar] Lightning has **one** dropper
(`Drop_Spirit_Thunder`) and **zero** ambient world placements — it is encounter/beacon-spawned only — and
Lightning is Stormseeker's element. Water has **no** droppers at all. Beside the creatures, a Lightning
field is a claim the shipped game contradicts across large regions. Upstream of them, it is a field whose
creatures are *not built yet*, which is a coherent thing for a mod to say and a natural questline hook.

A fourth, weaker corroboration: Void's droppers span 16 environments across three zones with no terrain
character, which independently supports §6's reading of Void as an **incursion** rather than a feature of
ground — a decision taken on lore and since corroborated by placement data. [jar]

## The known tension, recorded rather than resolved

Strict terrain derivation **will disagree with shipped placement in specific places**. [jar] Fire droppers
leak into `Zone1_Azure`, `Zone1_Swamps`, `Zone4_Forests` and `Zone4_Sewers`; Ice droppers appear in
`Zone3_Caves_Volcanic_T2/T3`. Under the old framing that was a cosmetic mismatch. Under this one the
field claims to *explain* those creatures, so a Fire dropper standing where volcanic-derived residue is
absent is the fiction failing its own promise.

**Accepted, for a reason that is itself lore.** Lore describes creatures as *dwelling* and *wandering*,
not as being generated by the ground beneath them — so a creature away from its field reads as a creature
that moved, which is the ordinary case for a living thing. The field explains *why a population is
there*, not why every individual is.

This is written down because the alternative is discovering it in play and mistaking it for a bug.

## Explicitly deferred — do not settle this here

**Whether derivation keys on environment ids or on terrain features.** The spawner keys on
`Environments`, a categorical label; §5's table keys on terrain character (lava, rivers, stone, heights).
They correlate — which is why the proxy works — but they are not the same signal, and the leakage above is
exactly where they come apart. Keying on environment ids would make agreement total by construction;
keying on terrain features keeps the field's character physical.

That is a **mechanism** question, not a fiction one. It belongs to the derivation work, with §9 q6
(*"does placement need a terrain scan, and at what cost"*) and §4's *"the lattice proposes, terrain
disposes"*. It was raised during this decision and deliberately not folded into it: this decision is
explicitly no-mechanism-change, and settling a derivation signal under that heading would smuggle one in.

## Operating calibration — faithful, not a straitjacket

Stated by the operator when taking this decision, and it governs how strictly the framing above should be
read:

> The mod should **feel like it belongs in Hytale**. Hytale is still in development — new items, systems
> and possibly lore will ship — and the intent is to adapt as it does while staying *fairly* faithful,
> without rigidity.

Two consequences, which the decision above is written to respect:

- **The test is "belongs", not "provably derivable".** A standard of total derivability gets stricter
  every time the game ships something our notes have not caught up with, and our notes go stale: the
  setting brief asserted *"There are no runes"* as a verified `[gap]` — true of `0.5.9`, false of
  `0.7.2`. Rigidity, not invention, is the failure mode this project has actually suffered.
- **Where fidelity and coherence pull apart, fidelity wins.** The game ships four non-matching elemental
  rosters ([`setting brief`](../../setting/hytale-orbis-setting-brief.md) §3). A maximally *coherent*
  answer tidies them into one list, and that tidying produced both prior wrong roster conclusions. We
  inherit the mess rather than resolving it on the game's behalf.

## What this unblocks

§9 q5's precondition. The roster decision — Gaia's cosmological five versus the six shipped essence
items — named *"settle §5's two-arrow chain first"* as a prerequisite, on the grounds that what the
derived field **means** must be fixed before the roster it is keyed to is. That prerequisite is now
satisfied. The roster decision still has **no code cost and no deadline**.
